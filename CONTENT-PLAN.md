# Content and contact plan

Primary audience: US, followed by UK and individual European markets. Main site intent: calculate CPM and related advertising metrics.

## Email and contact

Recommended first option: Zoho Mail's custom-domain free plan, if offered in the account's data center. Its advertised limits are one domain, up to five users and 5 GB per user. IMAP/POP/ActiveSync are not included. The owner should create and retain the account and mailbox login, for example contact@calculatethecpm.net. That address has not been created or verified by this repository.

Cloudflare Email Routing is a forwarding alternative to an existing inbox. It is not itself a separate mailbox login.

Once a real mailbox exists: verify the domain using the email provider's exact DNS records; configure its MX, SPF and DKIM records; verify inbound/outbound mail; configure a FormSubmit.co endpoint; activate it from the recipient inbox; enable spam protection; then test a real form submission with owner authorization. Update privacy disclosures to reflect the actual providers and data flow. Never publish credentials in this repository.

The old form targeted YOUR_EMAIL@GMAIL.COM. It is now explicitly unavailable instead of pretending to deliver messages. The contact page needs a verified recipient before it can be enabled. No contact email was sent during this update.

## Article workflow

1. Select one distinct reader question. Calculator pages target tool intent; articles target explanation and diagnosis.
2. Research current official platform definitions. Record URLs and dates for quantitative claims. Do not invent benchmark datasets.
3. Write original explanations, reproducible worked examples and a useful table or diagram where appropriate.
4. Add a concise opening answer, clear headings, a relevant calculator link, and only genuinely useful FAQs.
5. Check arithmetic, factual claims, title/description, mobile layout and internal links. Use a real author identity only with the owner's confirmation; never fabricate credentials.
6. Publish a blog index when there are complete articles to list. Do not deploy an empty blog for appearance alone.
7. Track indexing and query/page impressions, clicks and CTR in Search Console. Segment by US, UK and specific European countries; refine topics from actual queries.

AI can assist drafting, but each article needs factual review and original value. Avoid bulk repetitive country pages and unsupported annual rate tables.

## AdSense readiness

About Us explains the site's purpose and methods; it is useful for transparency but is not an approval guarantee. The current About page avoids invented company, team, and professional claims. A blog, paid mailbox, or a specific article count does not automatically secure approval.

Google's published eligibility emphasizes original, useful content and policy compliance. A privacy policy must accurately disclose applicable advertising cookies and providers. Existing legal policy text was preserved during the visual cleanup and still needs to be checked against the site's actual data practices before ads are enabled. Account-side consent settings for the target markets must also be addressed when activating ads.
