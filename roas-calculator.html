How to Calculate ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)?

Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) is a marketing metric that measures the efficacy of a digital advertising campaign. It determines how much gross revenue your business earns for every dollar spent on advertising.

Standard ROAS Formula Block
ROAS = Total Campaign Revenue ÷ Total Ad Spend
