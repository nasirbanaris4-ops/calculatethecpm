What is Return On Ad Spend (ROAS)?

Return On Ad Spend (ROAS) is a financial effectiveness metric that measures the gross revenue generated for every dollar spent on an advertising campaign. Expressed as a multiplier (e.g., 4x) or a percentage (e.g., 400%), ROAS evaluates campaign profitability across Meta, Google Ads, and e-commerce marketing.

How to Calculate ROAS (Formula & Steps)

Calculating ROAS requires dividing your gross sales revenue generated from ads by your total ad spend. To evaluate top-of-funnel view costs, use our master CPM Calculator, or analyze per-click acquisition costs with our CPC Calculator. The standard ROAS formula is:

ROAS Ratio = Total Ad Campaign Revenue / Total Ad Spend

Real Campaign Example: An e-commerce store spends $500 on ads and generates $2,000 in gross sales revenue. The calculation is:

ROAS Ratio = $2,000 ÷ $500 = 4.0x (or 400% Return)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Return On Ad Spend (ROAS)?

Return On Ad Spend (ROAS) is a marketing metric that measures the gross revenue generated for every dollar spent on an advertising campaign. Formula: ROAS = Gross Revenue / Total Ad Spend.

What is considered a good ROAS ratio?

A good ROAS ratio depends on profit margins. An average benchmark for e-commerce campaigns is 4:1 ($4 revenue for every $1 spent), while high-margin SaaS businesses may operate profitably at 2.5:1.

What is the difference between ROAS and ROI?

ROAS measures gross revenue generated strictly against direct advertising spend. ROI (Return On Investment) calculates overall net profit after deducting all business operational costs, shipping, and COGS.

What is breakeven ROAS?

Breakeven ROAS is the exact return ratio where your advertising revenue equals your combined product costs and ad expenses, resulting in zero net profit or loss.

How can advertisers improve campaign ROAS?

Advertisers can improve ROAS by increasing landing page conversion rates, raising Average Order Value (AOV) with upsells, refining audience targeting, and pausing low-performing ad creatives.
