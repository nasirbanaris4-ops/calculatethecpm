What is ROAS?

ROAS (return on ad spend) is attributed revenue divided by ad spend. A result of 4x means $4 of revenue per $1 of ad spend, equivalent to 400%.

See what every ad dollar returns

ROAS formula and example, with the formula shown below.

ROAS = Attributed revenue ÷ Ad spend

Worked example

A campaign generates $2,000 in attributed revenue from $500 in ad spend. ROAS is $2,000 ÷ $500 = 4x (400%). A 4x target needs $4,000 in revenue for $1,000 in spend.

These figures illustrate the arithmetic. They are not market averages or guaranteed campaign results. Volume forecasts can contain fractions; actual clicks and impressions are whole counts.

ROAS measures revenue return, not profit

Product costs, refunds, fulfillment, taxes, and overhead are not included in ROAS. Use revenue and spend in the same currency and align the attribution window. When entering a target, use a multiple such as 4, not the percentage 400.

Frequently asked questions

Should I enter 4 or 400 for a 400% ROAS?

Enter 4. The tool uses a revenue multiple, so 4x equals 400%.

Is a 1x ROAS break-even?

It means attributed revenue equals ad spend. It does not account for other business costs, so it is not necessarily profit break-even.

How do I calculate a budget from target ROAS?

Divide attributed revenue by the target ROAS multiple. For $2,000 in revenue at 4x ROAS, the budget is $500.

Can I use this calculator in any country?

Yes. The formulas work in any country. Choose your currency, enter all monetary values in it, and remember that the selector does not convert exchange rates.
