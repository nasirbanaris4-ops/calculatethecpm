What is Facebook Ads CPM?

Facebook Ads CPM (Cost Per Mille) measures the total cost incurred to display an advertisement 1,000 times across Meta platforms, including Facebook Feed, Instagram Reels, Stories, and Audience Network. CPM serves as Meta's primary auction pricing unit for bidding competition.

How to Calculate Facebook Ads CPM (Formula & Steps)

Facebook Ads CPM is calculated by dividing your total Meta campaign spend by the total number of ad impressions delivered, then multiplying by 1,000. You can also evaluate overall ad benchmarks on our general CPM Calculator. The standard Meta CPM formula is:

Facebook CPM = ( Total Meta Ad Spend / Total Impressions ) × 1,000

Real Campaign Example: An e-commerce brand spends $1,000 on Facebook Feed ads that deliver 80,000 impressions. The calculation is:

Facebook CPM = ($1,000 ÷ 80,000) × 1,000 = $12.50

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good CPM for Facebook Ads?

A good Facebook CPM in Tier-1 regions typically ranges between $7 and $15 for general e-commerce and consumer audiences. B2B, real estate, and financial campaigns often see higher CPMs ranging from $20 to $40+.

Why are my Facebook Ads CPMs so high?

High Facebook CPMs are usually caused by narrow audience targeting, low ad relevance diagnostics, intense auction competition during peak shopping seasons (like Q4), or creative fatigue.

How can I lower my Facebook Ads CPM?

You can lower your Facebook CPM by broadening your target audience, testing dynamic creative variations, improving ad engagement through better hooks, and using Advantage+ placements.

Does Facebook charge per click or per impression?

By default, Meta charges advertisers based on impressions (CPM). Even if you choose link clicks (CPC) as your optimization goal, Meta enters your ad into auctions using calculated CPM bids.

What is the difference between CPM and Frequency on Facebook?

CPM measures the cost per 1,000 total ad impressions, while Frequency measures the average number of times a single user sees your ad. High frequency alongside rising CPM indicates audience fatigue.
