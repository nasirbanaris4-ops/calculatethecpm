What is Facebook Ads CPM?

Facebook Ads CPM is ad spend per 1,000 impressions. Calculate it as spend divided by impressions, multiplied by 1,000. The same calculation works for Instagram ad reports.

See what your Meta ad costs per 1,000 impressions

Facebook Ads CPM formula and example, with the formula shown below.

Facebook Ads CPM = (Ad spend ÷ Ad impressions) × 1,000

Worked example

If a campaign spends $750 for 100,000 impressions, CPM is $7.50. At a fixed $7.50 CPM, 200,000 impressions would cost $1,500.

These figures illustrate the arithmetic. They are not market averages or guaranteed campaign results. Volume forecasts can contain fractions; actual clicks and impressions are whole counts.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use this calculator for Instagram?

Yes. Enter Instagram ad spend and impressions from the same campaign and reporting period.

Should I use reach instead of impressions?

No. CPM uses impressions. Reach counts unique people or accounts and produces a different metric.

Why can CPM change with the same budget?

Auction competition, audience, placements, creative, and delivery conditions can change. The calculator uses your supplied values rather than predicting auction prices.

Can I use this calculator in any country?

Yes. The formulas work in any country. Choose your currency, enter all monetary values in it, and remember that the selector does not convert exchange rates.
