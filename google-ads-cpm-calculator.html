Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is a good average CPM for Google Ads?

Average Google Display Network (GDN) CPMs typically range between $2.00 and $5.00 depending on your target industry and audience location. YouTube ads generally range higher, from $3.50 to $10.00 CPM.

How does Google calculate Display CPM?

Google calculates CPM by dividing your total ad campaign spend by the total number of ad impressions served, then multiplying that figure by 1,000.

How do I convert Google Display CPM to Cost Per Click (CPC)?
