What is Google Ads CPM?

Google Ads CPM (Cost Per Mille) measures the price an advertiser pays per 1,000 impressions on the Google Display Network (GDN) and Google Video Partners. Google Ads supports both standard CPM and viewable CPM (vCPM) bidding to optimize brand awareness campaigns.

How to Calculate Google Ads CPM (Formula & Steps)

Google Ads CPM is calculated by dividing total campaign ad spend by total banner or video impressions delivered, then multiplying by 1,000. You can also evaluate general advertising benchmarks on our master CPM Calculator. The standard Google CPM formula is:

Google CPM = ( Total Campaign Spend / Total Impressions ) × 1,000

Real Campaign Example: A company spends $500 on a Google Display campaign that delivers 62,500 impressions. The calculation is:

Google CPM = ($500 ÷ 62,500) × 1,000 = $8.00

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good CPM for Google Ads?

A good CPM on the Google Display Network ranges between $2.50 and $6.00 for standard display banners in Tier-1 countries. Video campaigns (vCPM) on Google Partner sites typically average between $6.00 and $14.00.

How does Google Display CPM compare to Search Ads CPC?

Google Display Ads focus on brand awareness using low-cost CPM bidding ($2-$6 per 1,000 views), whereas Google Search Ads target high purchase intent using Cost Per Click (CPC) bidding, which often costs $2 to $10+ per individual click.

Why are my Google Ads CPM rates increasing?

Google Ads CPM rates increase due to low Quality Scores, tight placement targeting, high auction competition during Q4 holiday periods, or bidding on competitive keyword topics.

How does Google calculate CPM in ad auctions?

Google determines auction winners based on your Maximum CPM bid combined with your Ad Quality Score and expected click-through rate across publisher websites.

What is vCPM in Google Ads?

vCPM (Viewable Cost Per Mille) is a Google Ads bidding strategy where you only pay when your ad is actually viewable on screen (defined as 50% of the ad visible for at least 1 second for display or 2 seconds for video).
