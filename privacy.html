Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This notice explains how CalculateTheCPM handles information when you use calculatethecpm.net and contact our support team. For privacy questions, email support@calculatethecpm.net.

Calculator inputs

Our calculators process the figures you enter in your browser. The calculator code does not send your campaign figures to us or save them in browser cookies or local storage. You do not need an account to use the calculators.

Hosting and security

Cloudflare hosts and protects this website. Delivering pages and preventing abuse involves processing technical information such as IP addresses, requested URLs, browser information and request times. IP addresses and similar identifiers may be personal data. Cloudflare may use security checks and necessary cookies to distinguish visitors from automated traffic. See Cloudflare's privacy policy and cookie information.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The current website code does not include Google Analytics, Google AdSense or advertising tracking tags. Our calculator code does not set advertising cookies. Hosting and security services may use necessary cookies as described above; this does not mean every visit receives a cookie.

If we introduce advertising or optional analytics, we will update this notice and provide any required privacy choices before activating technologies that require consent. Visiting this website does not by itself give consent to optional tracking.

Contact messages

When you submit the contact form, your name, email address, subject and message are sent to Web3Forms for processing and delivery to support@calculatethecpm.net. Web3Forms may also process technical information and perform spam checks. Submissions can be stored in its service as well as delivered by email. See Web3Forms' privacy policy.

Our support mailbox uses Zoho Mail. Messages sent directly by email are processed by your email provider and our mailbox provider. See Zoho's privacy policy. We use contact information to answer requests, investigate reported problems and prevent abuse. Sending a support request does not subscribe you to a marketing mailing list. Please do not include passwords, payment details or other sensitive information in a message.

Retention and your choices

We keep support messages while needed to handle the request and related follow-up, or meet applicable obligations. Copies may remain in provider storage or backups under their retention practices. You may ask about access, correction or deletion of your contact information by emailing support@calculatethecpm.net. We may need to verify that a request relates to you. Rights and exceptions depend on the laws that apply to your situation.

Service providers and international processing

The providers described above process information to deliver hosting, security, form handling and email services. Processing may take place outside your country. Their policies describe their locations, safeguards and retention practices. We do not sell the contact information you send us.

External links

Links to advertising platforms and other websites take you to services with their own privacy practices. Review their notices before sharing information with them.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above. Contact support@calculatethecpm.net with questions.
