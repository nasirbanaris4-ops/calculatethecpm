Privacy Policy

At calculatethecpm.net, accessible from https://calculatethecpm.net, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by calculatethecpm.net and how we use it.

Log Files & Analytics

calculatethecpm.net follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, calculatethecpm.net uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to calculatethecpm.net and other sites on the internet. However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL – https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Contact messages

If you use our contact form, your name, email address, subject and message are submitted to FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form delivery service, for delivery to support@calculatethecpm.net. FormSubmit may process technical information and use spam-prevention checks as part of this service. Review FormSubmit's privacy policy for its practices. If you email us directly, your email provider and our mailbox provider process that message instead.

We use contact information to respond to questions, feedback and calculation issues. Please send only information needed for your request. You can email support@calculatethecpm.net with questions about your contact message or to request its deletion, subject to applicable retention obligations.
