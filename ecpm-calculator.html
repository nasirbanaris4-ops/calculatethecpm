What is Effective Cost Per Mille (eCPM)?

Effective Cost Per Mille (eCPM) measures the actual ad earnings a digital publisher or mobile app owner makes for every 1,000 ad impressions served. Unlike advertiser-focused CPM metrics, eCPM aggregates revenue across multiple demand partners (Google AdSense, AdMob, header bidding) regardless of whether ads are paid per impression, click, or conversion.

How to Calculate eCPM (Formula & Steps)

Calculating eCPM requires dividing your total ad earnings by total ad impressions served and multiplying by 1,000. You can also evaluate advertiser view costs using our master CPM Calculator or analyze click rates using our CPC Calculator. The standard eCPM formula is:

eCPM = ( Total Ad Earnings / Total Impressions ) × 1,000

Real Publisher Example: A blog generates $1,200 in monthly AdSense revenue across 300,000 ad banner impressions. The calculation is:

eCPM = ($1,200 ÷ 300,000) × 1,000 = $4.00 Per 1,000 Impressions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is eCPM in digital publishing?

eCPM stands for Effective Cost Per Mille. It measures the total ad revenue a website publisher or mobile app developer earns for every 1,000 ad impressions delivered across their digital properties.

What is the difference between CPM and eCPM?

CPM (Cost Per Mille) represents the rate an advertiser pays for 1,000 ad views. eCPM (Effective Cost Per Mille) measures the actual revenue a publisher earns per 1,000 ad requests served across various ad networks.

How is eCPM calculated?

To calculate eCPM, divide your total ad earnings by total ad impressions served, then multiply the result by 1,000. Formula: eCPM = (Total Ad Revenue / Total Impressions) * 1,000.

What is considered a good eCPM rate for website publishers?

A good eCPM varies by ad format and audience region. Display banner eCPMs range between $1.50 and $4.00, mobile native ads range from $3.00 to $8.00, while rewarded video eCPMs in Tier-1 countries can exceed $15.00 to $30.00+.

How can website owners increase their eCPM?

Publishers can increase eCPM by optimizing ad placements above the fold, implementing header bidding header auctions, targeting high-RPM geographic regions, improving viewability scores, and using sticky ad units.
