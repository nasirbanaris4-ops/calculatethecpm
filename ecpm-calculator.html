What is eCPM?

eCPM (effective cost per mille) expresses ad revenue per 1,000 ad impressions. Divide ad revenue by impressions and multiply by 1,000, using the same reporting period.

See what your ad inventory earns per 1,000 impressions

eCPM formula and example, with the formula shown below.

eCPM = (Ad revenue ÷ Ad impressions) × 1,000

Worked example

A publisher earns $1,200 from 300,000 ad impressions. eCPM is ($1,200 ÷ 300,000) × 1,000 = $4. At that rate, 500,000 impressions would generate $2,000.

These figures illustrate the arithmetic. They are not market averages or guaranteed campaign results. Volume forecasts can contain fractions; actual clicks and impressions are whole counts.

Keep revenue and impression definitions consistent

Use the revenue basis you actually want to measure, such as net publisher ad revenue, and compare it consistently. Page views are not interchangeable with ad impressions: one page can show multiple ads. Forecast revenue assumes eCPM remains constant.

Frequently asked questions

How does eCPM differ from advertiser CPM?

Publisher eCPM measures realized ad revenue per 1,000 impressions. Advertiser CPM measures spend per 1,000 impressions. Fees and reporting definitions can make the values differ.

Can I use page views?

Use ad impressions for this calculation. Revenue per 1,000 page views is a separate page RPM metric.

Does the forecast include future demand changes?

No. It assumes the eCPM you enter stays constant and does not predict fill rate or auction demand.

Can I use this calculator in any country?

Yes. The formulas work in any country. Choose your currency, enter all monetary values in it, and remember that the selector does not convert exchange rates.
