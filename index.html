How to Calculate the CPM?

Cost Per Mille (CPM) measures the expense an advertiser pays for every 1,000 impressions or views an advertisement receives. Calculating CPM allows media buyers and publishers to compare cost efficiency across different advertising channels.

Standard CPM Formula Block
CPM = (Total Ad Spend ÷ Total Impressions) × 1,000

Where Total Ad Spend is in your local currency and Impressions represent raw ad views.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between CPM and RPM?

CPM is the cost advertisers pay per 1,000 views. RPM (Revenue Per Mille) is the net earnings creators receive after platform revenue shares (e.g. YouTube takes 45%).

Why do Tier-1 countries have higher CPMs?

Markets like the US, UK, Germany, and Australia have stronger consumer purchasing power, leading to higher advertiser competition for ad space.
