Understanding the CPM Formula

Cost Per Mille (CPM) represents the pricing metric for every 1,000 ad impressions delivered. The word "Mille" comes from Latin meaning thousand.

CPM = ( Total Ad Cost ÷ Total Impressions ) × 1,000
Calculate Ad Cost Cost = (CPM × Impressions) ÷ 1,000
Calculate Impressions Impressions = (Cost ÷ CPM) × 1,000
Convert CPM to CPC CPC = CPM ÷ (1,000 × CTR%)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is considered a good CPM rate?

A good CPM depends on target geography and industry niche. General display campaigns aim for $2.00–$5.00 CPM, while high-intent B2B or finance segments in Tier-1 countries can reach $30.00+.

What is the difference between CPM and CPC?

CPM charges per 1,000 ad views regardless of interactions, whereas CPC charges only when a user clicks your ad. Compare your click-versus-impression costs using our CPM vs CPC Converter.

How can I evaluate total campaign profitability?

While CPM measures top-of-funnel reach costs, total campaign return should be evaluated alongside conversion rates and revenue generated using our dedicated ROAS Calculator.
