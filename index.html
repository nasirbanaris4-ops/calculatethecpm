What is Cost Per Mille (CPM)?

Cost Per Mille (CPM) is a fundamental digital marketing metric that measures the cost of receiving 1,000 ad impressions. In Latin, "Mille" means thousand. Advertisers use CPM to evaluate the cost-efficiency of display ads, video campaigns, and social media promotions across networks like Google Ads, YouTube, Meta, and TikTok.

How to Calculate CPM (Formula & Examples)

Calculating CPM requires two key variables: your total campaign cost and total ad impressions. The standard mathematical formula is:

CPM = ( Total Ad Spend / Total Impressions ) × 1,000

Example Calculation: If an advertiser spends $250 on a Facebook ad campaign that generates 50,000 impressions, the CPM is calculated as follows:

CPM = ($250 / 50,000) × 1,000 = $5.00

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good CPM rate?

A "good" CPM depends on your industry and target country. In Tier 1 regions (US, UK, CA), a CPM between $5 and $12 is considered average for general audiences, while high-intent financial or B2B niches can exceed $30+ CPM.

What is the difference between CPM and eCPM?

CPM is the rate an advertiser pays for 1,000 ad views. eCPM (Effective Cost Per Mille) measures the total ad earnings a publisher makes per 1,000 total ad requests or impressions generated by their website.
