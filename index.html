What is Cost Per Mille (CPM)?

Cost Per Mille (CPM) is a digital marketing metric measuring the cost per 1,000 ad impressions ("Mille" means thousand). Advertisers use CPM to measure cost-efficiency across display, video, and social ad networks like Google Ads, YouTube, Meta, and TikTok.

How to Calculate CPM (Formula & Examples)

To calculate CPM, divide total ad spend by total impressions and multiply by 1,000:

CPM = ( Total Ad Spend / Total Impressions ) × 1,000

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good CPM rate?

In Tier 1 regions (US, UK, CA), CPMs between $5 and $12 are typical for general consumer audiences, while finance or B2B niches can exceed $30+.

What is the difference between CPM and eCPM?

CPM is the advertiser's cost per 1,000 ad views, whereas eCPM measures the ad publisher's earnings per 1,000 ad impressions on their site.
