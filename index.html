What is Cost Per Mille (CPM)?

Cost Per Mille (CPM) is a fundamental advertising metric that measures the cost an advertiser pays for 1,000 views or impressions of a ad campaign ("Mille" means thousand in Latin). CPM is widely used by media buyers to analyze cost-efficiency across networks like Google Display, Meta Ads, and video platforms.

How to Calculate CPM (Formula & Examples)

Calculating CPM requires knowing your total advertising budget and total impression volume. If you need to convert CPM metrics to click costs, you can also use our specialized CPM to CPC Calculator. The standard mathematical formula is:

CPM = ( Total Campaign Spend / Total Impressions ) × 1,000

Real Campaign Example: An advertiser spends $800 on a campaign that receives 160,000 video views. The calculation is:

CPM = ($800 ÷ 160,000) × 1,000 = $5.00

Frequently Asked Questions

What is CPM in digital advertising?

CPM stands for Cost Per Mille, where 'Mille' is Latin for one thousand. It is a pricing metric representing the cost an advertiser pays for 1,000 ad views or impressions.

How do you calculate CPM manually?

To calculate CPM manually, divide your total ad spend by total impressions, then multiply the result by 1,000. Formula: CPM = (Total Spend / Impressions) * 1,000.

What is considered a good CPM rate?

A good CPM depends on the target country and audience niche. In Tier-1 regions like the US and UK, average CPMs range between $5 and $12 for general consumer audiences, whereas finance or technology CPMs can exceed $25.

What is the key difference between CPM and CPC?

CPM charges advertisers based on 1,000 ad impressions regardless of clicks, while CPC (Cost Per Click) charges advertisers only when a user actively clicks on the advertisement.

Why is tracking CPM important for marketing campaigns?

Tracking CPM allows marketers to measure brand awareness efficiency, compare ad performance across different ad networks, and optimize campaign budgets without overspending.
