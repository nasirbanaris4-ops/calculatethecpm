About CalculateTheCPM

CalculateTheCPM provides free tools for understanding advertising costs and campaign results. Our calculators cover CPM, CPC, CTR, eCPM, ROAS, and CPM-to-CPC conversion, alongside platform-specific CPM examples.

What you can do here

Calculate a missing campaign metric, inspect the formula behind a result, and compare budget scenarios. The tools support marketers, publishers, small business owners, and people learning how digital advertising metrics work.

How our calculators work

Each calculator shows its formula and a worked example. Calculations run in your browser. Currency options label monetary values in USD, GBP, or EUR; they do not perform exchange-rate conversions.

For example, spending $800 on 160,000 ad impressions gives a CPM of $5. The result describes the cost of ad delivery; it does not predict conversions or profit.

Our approach to accuracy

We check calculation modes with worked examples, including zero and invalid inputs. Results are rounded for display. Estimates depend on your inputs and assume the supplied rates stay constant.

Examples are illustrative rather than claimed market averages. Platform terms can differ, so use consistent definitions and reporting periods when entering data.

Editorial approach

Our explanations focus on clear definitions, reproducible calculations, and practical limitations. Platform-specific explanations link to official documentation where relevant. We distinguish advertiser costs from publisher revenue and avoid presenting estimates as guaranteed results.
