What is Click-Through Rate (CTR)?

Click-Through Rate (CTR) measures the proportion of users who click on a digital ad compared to the total number of users who viewed it. Expressed as a percentage, CTR is a critical measure of creative engagement and audience relevance across Google Ads, Meta, and email campaigns.

How to Calculate CTR (Formula & Steps)

Calculating CTR requires dividing total ad clicks by total ad impressions and multiplying by 100. To see how CTR impacts your impression and click costs, use our CPM to CPC Calculator or analyze cost-per-click rates using our CPC Calculator. The standard formula is:

CTR (%) = ( Total Ad Clicks / Total Impressions ) × 100

Real Campaign Example: An ad campaign receives 500 clicks from 25,000 ad impressions. The calculation is:

CTR (%) = (500 ÷ 25,000) × 100 = 2.00%

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Click-Through Rate (CTR)?

Click-Through Rate (CTR) is a digital marketing metric measuring the percentage of total ad viewers who click on an advertisement link. Formula: CTR = (Total Clicks / Total Impressions) * 100.

What is considered a good CTR for online ads?

A good CTR varies by platform. Search ads average 2% to 5% CTR, social feed ads range between 1% and 2.5%, while web display banners typically average 0.35% to 0.80%.

How does CTR impact ad costs and CPM?

A higher CTR signals strong ad relevance to ad platforms like Google and Meta. High CTR improves your Quality Score, leading to lower CPM and CPC rates in ad auctions.

Why is my ad Click-Through Rate low?

Low CTR is typically caused by weak ad copy, unengaging visuals, poor audience targeting, creative fatigue, or irrelevant call-to-action (CTA) buttons.

How can I improve my ad campaign CTR?

You can improve CTR by writing strong headline hooks, testing dynamic ad creative variations, adding clear CTA buttons, and narrowing audience targeting to high-intent users.
