What is CPC?

CPC (cost per click) is ad spend divided by clicks. It tells you the average amount spent for each ad click in a campaign.

See what each click costs you

CPC formula and example, with the formula shown below.

CPC = Ad spend ÷ Clicks

Worked example

A campaign spending $500 for 250 clicks has a CPC of $2. At the same CPC, a $1,000 budget buys an estimated 500 clicks.

These figures illustrate the arithmetic. They are not market averages or guaranteed campaign results. Volume forecasts can contain fractions; actual clicks and impressions are whole counts.

Connect click costs to business outcomes

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate spend from CPC?

Multiply CPC by clicks. A $2 CPC and 250 clicks imply $500 in spend.

What is the difference between CPC and CPM?

CPC expresses cost per click. CPM expresses cost per 1,000 impressions. They measure different parts of ad performance.

Can clicks be zero?

Cost per click is undefined when clicks are zero. The tool asks for a positive divisor instead of displaying an incorrect result.

Can I use this calculator in any country?

Yes. The formulas work in any country. Choose your currency, enter all monetary values in it, and remember that the selector does not convert exchange rates.
