What is Cost Per Click (CPC)?

Cost Per Click (CPC) is an online advertising metric measuring the exact dollar amount an advertiser pays every time a visitor clicks on an ad banner, search link, or social ad post. CPC is a cornerstone of performance marketing across Google Search, Bing Ads, Meta, and LinkedIn.
