How to Convert CPM to CPC?

To calculate CPC from CPM, you need your campaign's Click-Through Rate (CTR). High CTRs significantly lower your effective Cost Per Click when buying media on a CPM model.

CPM to CPC Conversion Formula
CPC = CPM ÷ (10 × CTR%)
