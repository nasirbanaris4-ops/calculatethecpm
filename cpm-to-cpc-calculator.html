How do you convert CPM to CPC?

Convert CPM to CPC by dividing CPM by 10 times CTR expressed as a percentage. For a $10 CPM and 2% CTR, CPC is $10 ÷ (10 × 2) = $0.50.

See what your CPM costs you per click

CPM to CPC formula and example, with the formula shown below.

CPC = CPM ÷ (10 × CTR%)

Worked example

With a $10 CPM and 2% CTR, 1,000 impressions cost $10 and generate 20 clicks. The equivalent CPC is $10 ÷ 20 = $0.50.

These figures illustrate the arithmetic. They are not market averages or guaranteed campaign results. Volume forecasts can contain fractions; actual clicks and impressions are whole counts.

Enter CTR as a percentage

Enter 2 for 2%, not 0.02. The conversion assumes CPM and CTR describe the same traffic. It calculates an effective CPC rather than a guaranteed auction bid. You can also solve CPM = CPC × CTR% × 10 or CTR% = CPM ÷ (CPC × 10).

Frequently asked questions

Why does the formula use 10?

CPM is per 1,000 impressions and CTR is a percentage. Dividing 1,000 by 100 gives 10, which converts the percentage into expected clicks per 1,000 impressions.

Can CPM be converted without CTR?

No. CPM alone does not say how many impressions generate clicks. You need CTR or matching impression and click totals.

What happens when CTR is zero?

CPC cannot be calculated from a positive CPM and zero CTR because there are no clicks to divide the spend by.

Can I use this calculator in any country?

Yes. The formulas work in any country. Choose your currency, enter all monetary values in it, and remember that the selector does not convert exchange rates.
