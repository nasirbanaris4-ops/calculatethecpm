Understanding CPM vs CPC

How do you convert CPM to CPC?

The mathematical formula to convert CPM to CPC is: CPC = CPM ÷ (1,000 × (CTR ÷ 100)). For example, if your CPM is $10 and your CTR is 2%, your CPC equals $0.50.

Should I choose CPM or CPC bidding for my ad campaigns?

Choose CPM bidding if your primary goal is brand awareness and visibility. Choose CPC bidding if you are focused on direct response, lead generation, or driving website conversions. You can also evaluate overall campaign returns using our ROAS Calculator.

How does CTR affect conversion costs?

A higher Click-Through Rate (CTR) lowers your effective Cost Per Click when buying impressions on a CPM basis. Increasing CTR allows you to generate more visits for the exact same impression cost.
