What is TikTok CPM?

TikTok CPM (Cost Per Mille) measures the advertising cost incurred to generate 1,000 video impressions on TikTok's For You Page (FYP). TikTok CPM rates allow digital marketers to plan vertical video ad budgets across In-Feed Ads, Spark Ads, and Brand Takeovers efficiently.

How to Calculate TikTok CPM (Formula & Steps)

TikTok CPM is calculated by dividing total campaign spend by total video impressions delivered, then multiplying by 1,000. You can also evaluate overall ad benchmarks on our core CPM Calculator. The standard TikTok CPM formula is:

TikTok CPM = ( Total TikTok Spend / Total Video Views ) × 1,000

Real Campaign Example: A brand spends $800 on a TikTok Spark Ad campaign that generates 160,000 impressions. The calculation is:

TikTok CPM = ($800 ÷ 160,000) × 1,000 = $5.00

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good CPM rate on TikTok?

A good TikTok CPM typically ranges between $4 and $10 for In-Feed video ads in Tier-1 countries. Spark Ads and targeted interest campaigns generally average between $6 and $12.

Is TikTok CPM cheaper than Facebook Ads?

Yes, TikTok CPM rates are generally 20% to 40% cheaper than Meta (Facebook and Instagram) CPM rates due to lower auction competition and high organic video distribution.

What is the minimum budget for TikTok Ads?

TikTok requires a minimum daily budget of $50 at the campaign level and $20 at the ad group level for standard self-service ad accounts.

How do Spark Ads affect TikTok CPM?

Spark Ads allow brands to boost organic TikTok posts. They usually achieve higher watch time and engagement rates, which can lower overall effective CPM over time.

What factors cause high CPMs on TikTok?

TikTok CPMs increase when targeting narrow demographic audiences, bidding during peak holiday shopping periods (Q4), or using weak creative hooks that result in low video retention.
