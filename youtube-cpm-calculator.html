What is YouTube CPM?

YouTube ad CPM is advertiser cost per 1,000 ad impressions. Divide ad spend by ad impressions and multiply by 1,000. Use ad impressions rather than total video views.

YouTube CPM formula and example

YouTube CPM = (Ad spend ÷ Ad impressions) × 1,000

Worked example

If advertisers spend $500 on 62,500 ad impressions, CPM is ($500 ÷ 62,500) × 1,000 = $8. This does not mean the creator receives $8 per 1,000 video views.

These figures illustrate the arithmetic. They are not market averages or guaranteed campaign results. Volume forecasts can contain fractions; actual clicks and impressions are whole counts.

YouTube CPM versus creator RPM

CPM describes advertiser spending before revenue sharing. RPM describes creator revenue per 1,000 video views after revenue sharing and can include revenue beyond ads. Total video views, monetized playbacks, and ad impressions are different denominators. This tool calculates impression CPM, not playback-based CPM or take-home earnings.

Reference: YouTube Help: ad revenue metrics.

Frequently asked questions

Can I estimate creator earnings using CPM alone?

No. Creator earnings also depend on monetization, revenue sharing, ad delivery, and other revenue sources. Use actual creator revenue and video views when calculating RPM.

Should I enter video views or ad impressions?

Enter ad impressions. A video view may show no ads or multiple ads, so total views cannot be substituted for ad impressions.

Is this tool affiliated with YouTube?

No. It is an independent calculator and does not access your YouTube account.

Can I use this calculator in the US, UK, and Europe?

Yes. Select USD for US dollars, GBP for British pounds, or EUR for euros. Use the same currency for all monetary inputs. The selector does not convert exchange rates.
