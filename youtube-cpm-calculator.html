Understanding YouTube CPM vs. RPM

While CPM represents what advertisers pay for 1,000 ad impressions, RPM (Revenue Per Mille) measures what creators actually earn per 1,000 total video views after YouTube deducts its 45% revenue share.

YouTube Creator RPM Formula
RPM = (Advertiser CPM × 0.55)

YouTube automatically retains 45% of long-form ad revenue while distributing 55% to Partner Program creators.
