Understanding YouTube CPM & Creator Earnings

YouTube CPM represents the price an advertiser pays for every 1,000 ad displays on a video. Creators earn a 55% share of total ad revenue generated through YouTube Partner Program monetization.

What factors influence YouTube CPM?

  • Geographic Region: Tier 1 countries (US, UK, CA) yield higher CPMs ($5–$20+).
  • Content Niche: Finance, Technology, and Business niches receive premium advertiser bids.
  • Viewer Demographics: Older, high-income audiences command higher ad rates.
