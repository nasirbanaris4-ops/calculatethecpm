What is YouTube CPM?

YouTube CPM (Cost Per Mille) represents the price advertisers pay to place 1,000 video ad impressions on YouTube channel content. While CPM reflects gross advertiser costs, content creators receive 55% of this revenue through YouTube's Partner Program (known as RPM).

How to Calculate YouTube CPM (Formula & Steps)

YouTube CPM is determined by dividing total campaign ad spend (or gross estimated ad earnings) by total monetized video playbacks and multiplying by 1,000. You can also analyze general ad rates using our primary CPM Calculator. The standard YouTube CPM formula is:

YouTube CPM = ( Total Ad Spend / Monetized Ad Views ) × 1,000

Calculation Example: If an ad campaign spends $500 across 62,500 monetized playbacks, the CPM is:

YouTube CPM = ($500 ÷ 62,500) × 1,000 = $8.00

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good CPM rate on YouTube?

A good YouTube CPM typically ranges between $6 and $15 for general entertainment channels in Tier-1 countries. However, specialized niches like finance, business, and tech can achieve CPMs exceeding $25 to $40.

What is the difference between YouTube CPM and RPM?

CPM (Cost Per Mille) is the gross rate advertisers pay for 1,000 ad views. RPM (Revenue Per Mille) is the actual net earnings a creator keeps per 1,000 total video views after YouTube takes its 45% revenue cut.

How does YouTube pay creators for ad views?

YouTube pays creators through Google AdSense based on ad impressions and ad clicks. Revenue is shared 55% to the creator and 45% to YouTube when viewers watch at least 30 seconds of an ad or click on it.

Why do YouTube CPM rates fluctuate throughout the year?

YouTube CPMs fluctuate due to advertiser seasonal demand. Ad spend peaks during Q4 (Black Friday and holiday shopping) leading to high CPMs, and typically drops in January at the start of Q1.

Do all YouTube video views generate ad revenue?

No, not all video views generate revenue. Only views where an ad is served (monetized playbacks) count toward ad earnings. Viewers using ad blockers or skipped ads under 30 seconds may not generate ad revenue.
